Hi there, I'm Marga — but you can call me MAGI. ✨

I am a working student, an IT problem-solver, and a passionate scriptwriter. Currently in my second year pursuing a Diploma in Information Technology at PUP Sto. Tomas Campus, I balance my tech education with my role as an Non-Voice ESL Curriculum Scriptwriter at Course Hero Philippines.

To me, coding and writing are two sides of the same coin: every project is just a story waiting to be told. I bring a strong foundation in database management (MySQL, PostgreSQL) and UI/UX design (Figma, Canva), backed by a Cisco Networking Academy certification in IT Customer Support Basics.

Most recently, I've been channeling my analytical and web development skills into my final project, "Sip and Pulse"—a comprehensive cafe management web solution featuring both a structured Admin Side and an interactive Client Side. It serves as a full-stack cafe ordering and management system complete with a customer-facing website, an admin dashboard, a MySQL database, and Power BI analytics integration.

Beyond the screen, my favorite ways to recharge are reading books and manhwas, binge watching anime and films, and getting some good sleep.
